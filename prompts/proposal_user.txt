# PROPOSAL TASK

Propose up to three distinct unordered Unit pairs.

For each pair:
1. identify two different Units from the supplied catalog;
2. state the expected order of their scalar effects under the fixed operation;
3. explain briefly why this comparison is worth examining for the current input;
4. cite the relevant supplied evidence.

Prioritize unresolved comparisons that could affect a declared top-k prefix
when a complete reference ranking is available. This is a priority, not a
hard restriction: new directions and pairs currently on the same side of a
budget boundary are allowed when motivated by the input or evidence.

You may revisit a registered comparison that still lacks current evidence.
If both endpoints were already observed, do not describe their known ordering
as a new prediction or expect it to consume another predictor query.

Where possible, provide at least three distinct unqueried endpoints across
the proposed pairs. Shared endpoints are allowed when scientifically
motivated. Do not manufacture a clique or force endpoint sharing merely to
increase the number of completed comparisons.

Do not select the final query batch. The application will choose at most
three new Unit queries from the grounded endpoints.

Do not infer that a larger single-Unit effect proves joint sufficiency,
redundancy, interaction, or real-world causality.
